# SEO Short Series (Hinglish): "SEO Badal Gaya Hai"

**Audience:** Indian small businesses, creators, marketers
**Format:** Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts, 45–60 sec each, Hinglish
**Voice:** ElevenLabs "Karan – Confident Content Creator" (Hindi, hot-take / tech-creator style), model `eleven_v4`
**Hook rule:** every episode opens with a question to the viewer and ends with a teaser for the next episode (series loop).

## Trend analysis: what's trending in India right now (Oct 2026)

| Trend | Why it matters for an Indian audience | Episode |
|---|---|---|
| Google AI Mode / AI Overviews, now in Hindi and other Indian languages | Answers show up without a click: "rank #1, still zero clicks". The goal now is to get **cited**, not just to rank | Ep 1 |
| Instagram as a search engine | Instagram reads captions, bios and **Reel audio**. Google has also started indexing public Instagram content | Ep 2 |
| YouTube Shorts = discovery, long video = trust | Shorts may favour fresh content over evergreen | Ep 3 |
| Search Live / voice search in Hindi | People search the way they talk, in Hinglish, by voice | Ep 4 |
| Search everywhere / AI visibility | AI picks answers from YouTube, Instagram, Google Business Profile and reviews | Ep 5 |
| Real content vs AI filler | Audiences reject fake visuals and automated brand voices; founder stories and real proof work | Ep 6 |

Caveat: most of these sources are marketing/agency blogs, not Google's or Meta's own data. Use the numbers carefully.

## Episode list

1. **"Rank #1, phir bhi click zero?":** Google AI Mode and getting cited ✅ done
2. **"Instagram ab Google hai":** Instagram SEO (name field, bio, caption, keywords spoken in the Reel)
3. **"Shorts vs Long video":** YouTube SEO 2026
4. **"Log type nahi, bolte hain":** Hindi/Hinglish voice search and Search Live
5. **"AI ko kaise pata chalega aap exist karte ho?":** search everywhere and Google Business Profile
6. **"AI content se log bore ho gaye hain":** the human-content advantage
