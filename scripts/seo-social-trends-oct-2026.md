# SEO Trends on Social Media — October 2026

## Suggested topics (trending now)

1. **Social media is the new search engine:** people research products on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Reddit before they ever open Google.
2. **Social SEO:** put your keywords in the hook, the caption, the on-screen text, and say them out loud.
3. **"Search everywhere" / AI visibility:** AI Overviews and ChatGPT now shape what gets seen, and they cite YouTube, Reddit and TikTok content.
4. **Human content wins:** founder stories, real proof and real replies beat AI-generated filler.
5. **Short video with depth:** short clips are turning into series and episodes, and longer explainers are gaining weight.

Other angles for future episodes: Reddit threads ranking in Google, LinkedIn as a B2B search engine, and optimizing YouTube chapters for AI answers.

---

## Voiceover script (~60 seconds)

**Title:** Your next customer isn't Googling you. Here's where they're searching.

Here's something most brands still miss. Your next customer probably isn't Googling you. They're searching TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Reddit.

In 2026, social media *is* a search engine. So here are three SEO moves that are trending right now.

One: social SEO. Put your main keyword in the first three seconds. Say it out loud, show it on screen, and repeat it in your caption. The algorithm reads, and it listens too.

Two: search everywhere. AI answers from Google and ChatGPT are pulling from YouTube videos, Reddit threads and TikToks. If you're not posting there, you're invisible to AI too.

Three: be real. Audiences are tired of AI filler. Founder stories, real results and real replies in the comments are what get saved, shared and ranked.

And don't stop at one video. Turn your best topic into a series, because episodes keep people coming back.

Rank on Google, yes. But show up everywhere your customer scrolls.

Follow for more SEO trends every week.
