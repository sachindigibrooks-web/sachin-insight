## Caption
When I built the Mudras BodyReset, I started with one simple idea: no two bodies show up the same way, so no two sessions should look the same.
Most studios sell time slots: pick 60 minutes and get the same routine as everyone else. We start with a conversation instead, about goals, work, training and how the body feels today. The techniques, pressure and focus follow from that.
After more than 25 years of bodywork with athletes, performers and executives, I've seen it again and again: the details matter most when someone gets attention as an individual.
It's a lesson that goes beyond massage. Where in your business could "bespoke" make the biggest difference?
— Yotam Kafri, Founder, Mudras
