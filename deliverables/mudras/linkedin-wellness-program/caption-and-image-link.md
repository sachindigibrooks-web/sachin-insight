## Caption
Hours of sitting and screen time often show up in the neck and shoulders first.

It's the most common thing we hear from professionals who walk into our Chelsea studio. It rarely comes from one bad day. It's the accumulation: back-to-back calls, travel, a laptop on the sofa at 10pm.

Pain is a lagging indicator. Tension builds for weeks before people notice it.

If you lead a team, it's worth asking: what does your wellness program do for the body, not just the mind?
