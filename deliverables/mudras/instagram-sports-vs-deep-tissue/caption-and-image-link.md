## Caption
Training for a marathon? Here's how runners can choose the right massage. Sports massage is planned around your training, race date and recovery, and can include deep tissue, stretching and mobility work. Deep tissue targets deeper muscle and connective tissue, but skip it in the final days before a race. Not sure? Your specialist builds the session around your mileage and how your legs feel.
