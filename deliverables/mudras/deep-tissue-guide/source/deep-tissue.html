Deep tissue massage
What it feels like
& how to prepare
01 · What it feels like
Slow, firm pressure.
It reaches the deeper layers of muscle a lighter massage can’t. Tight areas can feel intense, but intense isn’t the same as painful.
02 · Pressure
More pressure isn’t
always better.
Holding your breath or tensing up? That’s too much.
03 · Communicate
Talk to your
therapist.
Share what your body needs before you start. Too much? Say so in the moment instead of pushing through.
04 · Afterward
Mild soreness
is normal.
For a day or two,
like after a hard workout.
  • —Water, gentle movement and a warm shower can help
  • —Lasts more than a few days?
    Tell your specialist
05 · How to prepare
  • 01Complete your intake before you arrive
  • 02Arrive 5 minutes early
  • 03Mention tight spots and injuries
